Module 2 · UTMN study system

100-point rating system

You collect points all trimester for attendance, seminar work and checkpoints, not only at the final exam. With 61 points or more you can receive your grade automatically.

Credit (zachyot) vs. exam

Credit: taken in the last study week, graded “pass / fail”.

Exam: taken in the exam session (January / June) with a graded mark: excellent, good or satisfactory.

Academic etiquette

In Russia teachers are addressed by first name and patronymic (e.g. Anna Aleksandrovna). In classes taught in English use “Professor” or “Dear [Name]”. If you are ill, tell your teacher in advance.

Module 3 · Siberian winter & city services

The three-layer rule (−25 °C … −35 °C)

1. Base: synthetic or merino thermal underwear. Not cotton: it holds moisture and cools you down.

2. Middle: a fleece jacket or a wool sweater.

3. Outer: a windproof down coat below the knee and winter boots with thick, ridged soles.

Must-have apps

2GIS: offline city map with exact building entrances and bus routes.

Yandex Go: taxi and delivery.

VKontakte & Telegram: official chats of UTMN academic groups.

Apteka.ru: find medicines.

Emergency & health

Call 112, the single emergency number in Russia (English-speaking operators are available). Always bring your voluntary medical insurance policy (VHI) to the clinic.

COMPONENT B

“Global Buddy & UTMN Speaking Club”

A four-week mentoring programme. Each international first-year student is paired with a Russian buddy from years 1–2. Meetings take place in UTMN student spaces (Lenina St. 23 / Respubliki St. 9).

How the pairing works

Newcomer: asks questions, joins every meeting, shares their culture.

Buddy: shows the campus, explains Modeus and winter life, practises English with the newcomer.

Goal: every newcomer makes friends with at least 2–3 local students in the first month.

Four-week plan

Why it works

It is created by students, adapted to real life in Tyumen and needs no extra university budget. In a 2-week pilot (n = 10), newcomers’ average confidence rose from 2.18 to 4.65 out of 5.
